Biography

I'm Khushboo (she/her), a Growth Product Manager in Toronto with 10+ years across engineering, design, and product. I started my journey as a front-end developer at Digital Picasso, moved into product design - spending nearly six years building analytics experiences for global brands like McDonald's, Delhi Airport, Decathlon, and Honda - and then into product management. That trifecta shapes how I build: I understand how products get engineered, how they should feel, and what makes them grow. It's a holistic view of building product, end to end.

At President's Choice Financial, I rose from Digital Growth product roles to Digital Product Lead, owning acquisition, onboarding, and risk journeys for credit and debit products. I led the 0→1 launch of PC Financial's first fee-based credit card journey, shipped instant issuance that enabled 2,000+ activations, and cut key funnel drop-offs from ~8.1% to ~1.5%.

Today I lead acquisition & onboarding product at EQ Bank, with a growing focus on AI product management - grounded in judgment, guardrails, and evaluation thinking. I do my best work in regulated environments, where clarity, trust, and craft matter most.
